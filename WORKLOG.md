- 20:59 — Code committed in seven slices, one per area, in dependency order. Each commit was checked out on its own and its suite run green before the next: 15, 35, 49, 57, 64, 80, 80 tests. Design committed in sync with the code.
- 21:10 to 21:33 (ends at the two commits) — Stress test. A separate agent wrote seeded-random invariant checks over 6-, 30- and 90-day streams plus adversarial cases, under instruction to report and not fix. All documented invariants held; nine adversarial tests found real gaps: a settlement on one account could consume another account's hold; the hold was marked settled before the journal append could fail; a credit too small for its instalments was half-booked; a negative authorization was approved; days after the window never closed; a value date before Day 1 earned interest. Fixed by scoping holds to (account, auth id), deciding before appending and recording after, all-or-nothing postings, positive-amount checks at the boundary, closing trailing days, and clamping accruals at Day 1. Two document lines were wrong rather than the code: "one fee per day, ever" now reads "one standing fee", and auth-id idempotency is per account. The stress tests stay in the repository.
- 22:54 — Every operation is idempotent on its id: a second delivery is ignored with a warning, so the stream's two idempotency keys are now the operation id for everything and the auth id for authorizations.
